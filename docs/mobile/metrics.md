# Mobile measuring stick: definitions (M1.1 copies this to docs/mobile/metrics.md)

Everything a unit's acceptance measures is defined here once. `inputs/capture.js` and `inputs/probe.js` are the verified scratchpad harness that M1.1 promotes; `inputs/brief.md` is the research brief the reference numbers come from; `inputs/*-metrics.json` are the raw baseline captures (sandbox, the sandbox's Chromium (/opt/pw-browsers chromium-1194) under SwiftShader, Google Fonts and unpkg hosts blocked or stubbed). Where a number below differs from a fresh capture by more than 5%, the fresh capture is recorded as B0 and the unit text reads B0 (units never hard-code a sandbox number except as "was").

## 1. Counting rules (bytes, requests, foreign origins)
- `bytes` / `requests` / `max_asset` count only responses served by the app's own server (CDP `Network.loadingFinished.encodedDataLength`). Requests fulfilled by a Playwright route (the Leaflet and three.js stubs under `--cdn-dir`) and requests aborted by a route (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com) are excluded from all three and listed in `routed[]` / `aborted[]` with their fulfilled body length. This is how the reference numbers were taken: 729,636 B / 37 requests = index 211,860 + data JSON about 216 KB + tiles 289,647 (20 requests) + the rest; the Leaflet stub is NOT in it.
- `foreign_origins` = distinct origins other than the server's that were requested, routed or aborted. Today the atlas shows 3 (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com, unpkg.com) and the sim shows cdnjs.cloudflare.com plus the two Google hosts.
- Repeat visit method: the same browser context, first `page.goto(url)` settled (Contents shown, network idle + 400 ms), then a second `page.goto(url)` to the same URL (not `reload`, not a new context). `cache_hits` = requests that were answered 304, or reported by CDP `Network.requestServedFromCache`, or served by a worker. Reference: 0 hits.
- Byte targets in units are "transferred" bytes as above; "raw" means file size on disk.

## 4. Metric formulas
- `doc_scroll` = `document.scrollingElement.scrollHeight`; compared with `innerHeight`.
- `chrome_cover.<state>` = area of the union of the bounding rects of all visible fixed or absolute chrome elements (everything inside `body` that is not inside `#map .leaflet-pane`, intersected with the viewport) divided by viewport area. `pointer-events` never exempts an element: a positioned element counts when it paints (a non-transparent background, border, box-shadow, backdrop filter or image, a replaced or form control, or text of its own) or when it takes pointer events. Only wrappers that paint nothing and take no pointer events (the Leaflet corners, whose controls are counted themselves) and a pointer-events:none texture covering 90% or more of the viewport (the uncharted grain) are left out. Measured per state: whole (The Whole Chart, drawer closed or at peek as the unit says), layers (layers list open), card (the place card open), peek and half (the sheet driven to that stop, measured with the card open on the atlas and with the ledger open on the sim; the sim records `chrome_cover.ledger`, `.peek`, `.half`). The sheet is driven through the hook `window.ATLAS.sheet.stop('peek'|'half')` / `window.ANNALS.sheet.stop(...)`; the unit that builds a sheet provides it. A sheet exists when the surface exposes that hook, or a shown `[data-sheet-handle]`, `.sheet-handle` or `#sheetHandle` inside `#panel` (atlas) or `#drawer` (sim); without the hook the tool taps the handle. The hook is not trusted on its word: whichever drove it, a stop is measured only after the sheet reports that stop through `data-sheet-stop` / `data-stop` on its `[data-sheet]` element (or the root) or a class `peek|half|full`, `sheet-<stop>`, `is-<stop>`, and `half` only when the sheet's rect differs from its rect at `peek` (a sheet that did not move was not driven). A no-op hook, a sheet that reports no stop or another stop, or one that did not move records `'unmeasured: stop not reached (<detail>)'`. While a surface has no sheet the value is the string `'no-sheet'`; any other failure to measure is `'unmeasured: <reason>'`. The value is never null, so a broken probe differs from B0 and trips the unchanged rule, and an `--accept` check on a `.peek` or `.half` key fails unless the capture holds a measured number.
- `share_visible` = the visible fraction of the chart rectangle's width times height: intersection of the viewport (in atlas px through `map.getBounds()`) with the full chart rectangle, divided by the chart rectangle area, at The Whole Chart view.
- `contents.kickerTop` / `titleTop` = `getBoundingClientRect().top` of the Contents kicker and title (reference -26 and -8 on iphone13).
- `hover_ungated` = number of CSSOM style rules containing `:hover` that are NOT nested inside an `@media (hover:hover)` rule (walk `document.styleSheets[*].cssRules`; same-origin sheets only). Reference 29.
- `targets_under_44` = visible interactive controls (control inventory below) whose border box is below 44x44 CSS px; markers count by their pad size (the larger of glyph and pad rect). Ratio is that count divided by the inventory size. Reference 123 of 127 on iphone13.
- `min_font_px` = smallest computed `font-size` among visible text nodes of the chrome (not map tile art).
- `wrong_card` / `wrong_taps` = taps at the centre of each interactive glyph's drawn `.glyph` rect, at every view A-U3 names (the whole chart, Leponnia z3.5, Epēshu z4.55, Epēshu z4.95, city z6.15; absolute zooms, clamped to the profile's range, the reached zoom recorded), every glyph that is inside the viewport and not under chrome (`--tap-n N` samples N per view instead and marks the fixture `complete:false`). The map is put back to the view after each tap. Own-card is decided on whole names, never substrings: the name parsed from `#place=` / `#faction=` / `#company=` (or a card title, or a title segment before a dash) folded for case and diacritics must equal the glyph's name. Before folding, every name (glyph, hash, card title, chooser row) is normalised: Unicode NFC, provenance marks dropped (the card title is read from a clone of the `<h2>` without `<sup>` / `.prov-star`, and any `✶` plus a trailing run of marker glyphs is stripped from the text), whitespace collapsed; so the sea-lane card 'The Strait Run✶' is own for the glyph The Strait Run while 'The Strait Runway✶' is not (so Lepon is not Leponnia nor Lepon the Old, Epēshu is not The Senate of Epēshu); the one alias is an origin flag, 'Hometown of X' / 'Haunt of X', which opens `#company=X`. Wrong when the tap opens a card (or `location.hash`) other than that glyph's, or opens a chooser that is not shown or whose visible rows do not list the glyph by whole name or whole segment (`chooser_without_glyph`); a shown chooser whose rows list the glyph counts as right. A row's `opened` is the card hash (`#place=` / `#faction=` / `#company=`), else the card title as normalised above (marks dropped), else any other hash (`#view=` with no card open), else the chooser rows; own rows are not recorded, and the list keeps the first 60 of the rest. B0's `tap_fixture` was re-taken after the provenance-mark fix (two full atlas captures of B0's tree agreed exactly, and only `tap_fixture` was replaced; the starred sea-lane cards The Strait Run, The Gulf Crossing, The South Lane and The North Coast Packet are own), so B0's `wrong_card` figures are iphone13 15, pixel7 12, landscape 68, desktop 23, desktop2x 20 (were 18, 15, 70, 35, 33). `--self-test` re-runs the scorer over every `other` row recorded in `docs/mobile/baseline.json` whose `opened` is not a card hash and checks the counts add up, so B0 cannot disagree with the scorer again; a scorer change that moves a recorded row means re-taking B0's `tap_fixture` in the same change.
- UG2 error accounting: a generic `Failed to load resource` console line is explained only when its own URL (`ConsoleMessage.location().url`) is on a foreign host whose request this run aborted or failed; a local 404 is never explained. Every page the capture opens is read: the main page, the fingerprint page, and each extra (safe-area, keyboard, LoAF, real-clock, mutate) records its own `errors` under `extra_errors.<name>` (outside `timing`, outside the identity compare), and UG2 requires `unexplained` 0 in all of them.
- `share`/`phone.overlaps` follow the filigree capture contract.
- `calls`, `tris` = `renderer.info.render.calls` / `.triangles` at the street probe's three fixed views under the virtual clock.
- `fps_median` = median of the per-second frame counts over the report window, from the rAF timestamp argument only.
- `clock_tokens`: see section 6.
- `cityCanvasSha` = sha256 of the seeded city canvas bytes (`genCityCanvas` output) and must be equal across all five profiles.
- Fingerprint = sha256 of the street probe's canonical JSON (`street-1-research.js` PROBE_SPEC `--fingerprint`): `{clock: W.clock (every key), dayTicked, settlements: [{id|name, pop, kind, prosperity}], agents: [{kind, departDay, route: src>dst, speed}], chron: the W.chron texts, treasury, routeVolume}`, numbers rounded to 6 places, taken for `#s=epeshu` and `#s=tamar1374` in a fresh page of its own (UG3 always requires both seeds on every UG3 profile: the seed list is the union of these two, the baseline's and the capture's, a seed the capture lacks or that errored fails `UG3.sim.<profile>.<seed>`, a declared `sim.<p>.seeds` key does not excuse it, and `--accept` refuses a `--seeds` override that drops either), before any UI probe, hidden-tab test or camera view: load, speed 0, `ANNALS.hold(1e12)`, `ANNALS.simDays(400)`, fingerprint, `rng_next`. Counts alone never stand in for contents: the sensitivity test (`--self-test`) shifts the first caravan's `departDay` by 1, changes a chronicle text, and applies the probe's `--perturb departDay`, and the sha must change each time. A unit that changes what happens before the fingerprint has nothing to declare: UG3 cannot be waived for it.

## 5. Control inventory, reach, primary actions
- Inventory = every element matching `a[href], button, input, select, textarea, summary, [role=button], [tabindex]:not([tabindex="-1"]), .leaflet-control a, .leaflet-marker-icon.leaflet-interactive` that is visible and hit-testable in some state reachable from the initial view. A control's id is `role + ":" + accessible name` (aria-label, else title, else text trimmed to 24 characters).
- `docs/mobile/controls.json` is captured on `desktop` (1366x768) in B0 and lists every id with `primary:true|false`.
- `reach` of a control on a phone = the smallest number of taps (BFS over disclosure controls: `aria-expanded`, drawer handle, layers toggle) after which `document.elementFromPoint` at its centre returns it. A-U8 requires reach <= 2 for every id in controls.json.
- Primary actions (`primary:true`): atlas = search field, zoom in, zoom out, drawer handle, layers toggle, card close, card first action; sim = pause, the five speed buttons, Hide the court, Return the court, the ledger open control, Close the chart. "Bottom 25%" means the control's centre lies in the bottom quarter of `innerHeight`.

## 6. Clock tokens
- Pattern P = `Math[.]random|Date[.]now|new Date[(][)]|performance[.]now`; wide pattern W = `Math[.]random|Date[.]now|new Date[(]|Date[(]|Date[.]parse|performance[.]now|Intl[.]DateTimeFormat|crypto[.]getRandomValues|requestIdleCallback`.
- `clock_tokens` is counted by OCCURRENCES (`grep -oE ... | wc -l`), not lines. Baseline (this tree): `index.html` P = 37 occurrences on 35 lines, W = 37; `maps-site/index.html` P = 1, W = 2 (`requestIdleCallback` at about line 2010 and `performance.now` at about line 3181).
- The gate is: occurrences in `index.html` stay exactly 37 (P and W) and the per-line multiset of matched tokens (line text, token, count) is identical to B0 apart from lines a unit declares; maps-site stays at its W baseline. The count is always checked. `declared_clock_lines` entries are whole lines, keyed on the sha of the whole trimmed line (a substring never covers a line, and two lines that share their first 160 characters are different lines); each must exist in B0 or in the capture. A declaration has a multiplicity: each listing covers ONE changed copy of that line (added, removed or re-indented, summed over both files, per pattern). If more copies changed than the line is listed, the gate fails (`UG3.declared_clock_lines.P|W`). Only the covered copies are removed before the count and the multiset are compared, so every other occurrence, including further copies of a declared line, still counts against B0 (with nothing declared, exactly 37). The ladder, the hidden-pause handler and the wake-lock code take time only from the rAF timestamp argument.

## 7. Image diff and desktop identity
- One function, always at full css-px resolution: decode both PNG shots at the same size; a pixel differs when `max(|dR|,|dG|,|dB|) > 8`; `diff` = differing pixels outside the declared regions divided by all pixels. Default tolerance 0.5%. Declared regions are rectangles in the accept file (`mask`). Shots are taken with `animations:'disabled'`, `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, the virtual clock frozen, and tile layers settled.
- "Identical" for computed-style or DOM snapshots = deep equality of `{selector, rect, computed font-size, color, display, visibility}` records for the control inventory, ignoring keys the accept file lists under `declared_change_keys`.
- A shot over 300 KB is stored twice: the box-reduced copy (`scale` < 1, for viewing) and the css-px original beside it as `<name>.full.png` (`full_path`; both under the git-ignored `docs/mobile/shots/`). Each shot record holds the sha256 of the files written (`sha`, and `full_sha` for the original); before diffing, both the reference's and the capture's files are re-hashed and a missing sha or a mismatch fails the diff as "shot changed since capture" (recapture), so a later run that overwrites a file name cannot stand in for the reference. A unit writes to `docs/mobile/shots/<unit>/`; every other run (B0, R1, final, ad hoc) writes to `docs/mobile/shots/<output name or adhoc>-<tree sha>/`, never to a dir another output shares. The diff reads the original; a reduced shot without a `full_path` fails the diff (recapture), because averaging blocks hides thin changes (1-px hairlines darkened by 24 levels differ on 17% of pixels at full size and on 0% at 1/3).
- Desktop identity (UG8) runs on desktop and desktop2x. Its shot diff covers the union of the views the baseline holds, the views the accept file requires (`shot_views`, and every view named by a `<surface>.<profile>.shot_diff.<view>` check) and the views the capture holds, not only the capture's. A conditional shot (`3-layers` when the layers control is found, `2-ledger` when the ledger opens) that the capture lacks fails with diff 1 and the reason `missing from the capture (required by the reference|accept file)`; a view missing from the reference fails as `missing from the reference`. A `shot_diff.<view>` check on a view the capture lacks fails the same way.

## 8. The `--accept` file
`docs/mobile/accept/<unit-id>.json`. Authored BEFORE the implementer runs, by the loop's accept-author step (a sonnet/medium agent that sees only the unit's acceptance bullets and this file, never the implementer's diff) or by the central session; the implementer receives it read-only. M1.1 delivers `--accept` scoring and `--lint-accept <file>` (schema check).

```json
{
  "unit": "A-U1",
  "profiles": ["iphone13", "pixel7", "landscape", "desktop", "desktop2x"],
  "clock": "virtual",
  "gates": ["UG1","UG2","UG3","UG4","UG5","UG6","UG7","UG8","UG9","UG10","UG11"],
  "declared_change_keys": ["atlas.iphone13.doc_scroll", "atlas.pixel7.doc_scroll"],
  "mask": [{"profile": "desktop", "rect": [0, 0, 0, 0], "why": "none"}],
  "checks": [
    {"key": "atlas.iphone13.doc_scroll", "op": "==", "value": 664, "was": 974},
    {"key": "atlas.iphone13.contents.kickerTop", "op": ">=", "value": 0, "was": -26},
    {"key": "atlas.desktop.shot_diff", "op": "<=", "value": 0.005, "ref": "R1"},
    {"key": "atlas.desktop.metrics", "op": "deep-equals", "ref": "R1", "except": ["declared_change_keys"]}
  ]
}
```
`--lint-accept` rejects a file whose `profiles` do not cover its listed gates: UG3 needs all five (iphone13, pixel7, landscape, desktop, desktop2x), UG2 needs iphone13, pixel7, desktop, desktop2x; scoring also fails a gate whose profile is missing from the capture. Optional `shot_views` (array of view names) adds views UG8 must diff beside the baseline's. Ops: `==`, `!=`, `<`, `<=`, `>`, `>=`, `between` (value `[lo,hi]`), `within` (value, `tol` as a fraction), `deep-equals`, `absent`. `ref` names a baseline (`B0`, `R1`, `final`, or a unit id's capture); a check with `ref` and no `value` compares to that baseline's same key. A key not listed in `declared_change_keys` must equal its B0/R1 value or the unit fails. A declared key names a concrete metric: `surface.profile.metric` at least, with no `*` in the surface, profile or last segment (`atlas.*.*` and `atlas.iphone13.*` are rejected by `--lint-accept`); a `*` may stand only for a whole middle segment. Output: `{unit, pass, failing:[{key, op, expected, got}]}` and exit code 1 on any failure.

## 9. Voice rubric (UG9)
`docs/mobile/voice-rubric.md` is the only rubric. Player-visible strings (labels, titles, aria-labels, hint text, tidings) are enumerated in `docs/mobile/README.md` under "Player-visible strings"; only that list is voice-read. A string passes when: it matches neither `VOCAB` nor `VOCAB_ST` (`VOCAB` is the const at `.claude/workflows/filigree-1-research.js:97`; `VOCAB_ST` is derived from it in the street config region and read by `tools/street-drift.js:138`; the voice read runs the regex from those files, it does not retype it); it contains none of: tap, click, swipe, pinch, drawer, modal, menu, toggle, dismiss, app, download, install, offline mode, dark mode, theme, settings; years appear only as A.B.; the words are table words (chart, court, ink, lit, keep, furl, mark). The wiki canon names (for example "The Lektān Priesthood", a faction name) are exempt from the VOCAB grep and from the jargon list. A sonnet/low reader returns one pass/fail per string with the offending word; the unit passes with 0 fails.

## 10. The keydown sha
Range: the text of `index.html` from `window.addEventListener('keydown'` to its matching `});`, found by a node script that matches braces starting at the first `{` after the anchor (the definition Street 1's `keydown_sha` uses, `street-1-research.js` line 588); sha256 of that text.
